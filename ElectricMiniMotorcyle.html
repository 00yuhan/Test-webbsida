BUILDING THE MINIMOTO


Coming from a background in Industrial Design Engineering, crossing over into the mechanical side was a refreshing change of pace. There was something very satisfying about being in the workshop: standing at the lathe to turn the head tube, hand-fitting the bearings, and watching the water cutter translate CAD models into real components. Seeing an idea transform into a fully-fledged, working mini-motorbike confirmed why I love this field. It showed how people with different interests and knowledge, but the same goal, can come together and create something awesome, and lastly how important and fun it is to be hands-on and create real things.
